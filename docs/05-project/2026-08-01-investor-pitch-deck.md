## Slide 1 — House of Veritas

### Secure estate management and digital governance for complex households and property employers

**One platform for compliance, operations, and audit-ready accountability.**

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

Estate operations are still managed across paper files, spreadsheets, messaging
apps, and disconnected tools.

- Administrative overload
- Missed deadlines and document expiries
- Limited visibility and accountability
- Slow incident response
- Compliance and audit gaps

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

House of Veritas brings essential estate workflows into one secure, structured
platform.

- Records and approvals
- Staff administration
- Tasks and incidents
- Assets and vehicles
- Expenses and oversight
- Role-based access, e-signatures, and audit trails

---

## Slide 4 — Why Now

Private estates face rising operational complexity and growing compliance
expectations.

- More distributed teams
- Higher service expectations
- Greater demand for documented accountability
- Practical mobile-first workflows
- Accessible e-signatures and audit trails
- Cost-effective AI-assisted guidance

---

## Slide 5 — Beachhead Market

### Start where operational complexity creates recurring governance risk

**Initial market hypothesis:** South African owner-managed private households and
small private estates that have:

- two or more recurring staff members or service providers;
- recurring staff, document, asset, incident, or approval obligations;
- operations coordinated across messaging, paper, and spreadsheets; and
- an owner or delegated manager with authority to adopt and fund a solution.

The precise staff and complexity threshold will be validated through customer
discovery and pilots. Family offices and multi-property portfolios are potential
later expansion segments, not the initial proof point.

---

## Slide 6 — Product Strategy

Start with high-friction compliance workflows, then expand into the operating
system for estate administration.

- Document tracking and expiry alerts
- Staff onboarding, records, and policies
- Incident reporting and approvals
- Asset and vehicle management
- Expense logging and oversight
- Permissions, signatures, and audit history

---

## Slide 7 — Product Workflow

### One reviewable operational trail

**Illustrative workflow**

`Capture issue or obligation` → `Assign owner` → `Guide and approve action` →
`Verify completion` → `Retain audit history`

The workflow brings together:

- mobile evidence capture;
- role-appropriate tasks and instructions;
- human-reviewed AI-assisted guidance;
- approvals, escalation, and e-signatures; and
- completion, reopening, and audit records.

This is an illustrative workflow view, not a claim that every displayed module
has completed pilot validation.

---

## Slide 9 — Business Model

Subscription SaaS with clear expansion potential.

- Base platform subscription
- Pricing by staff count, estate complexity, or modules used
- Premium reporting, AI guidance, and governance controls
- Optional onboarding and implementation services

Pricing and packaging remain hypotheses until tested through pilot commitments
and priced continuation offers.

---

## Slide 10 — Competitive Position

House of Veritas is designed to combine operational coordination with
governance-grade accountability.

| Alternative                  | What it does well                                    | Typical gap House of Veritas tests                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spreadsheets and paper       | Flexible, familiar, inexpensive                      | Weak ownership, reminders, permissions, and audit history                      |
| Messaging apps               | Fast communication and broad adoption                | Decisions, evidence, and completion history become fragmented                  |
| HR and payroll tools         | Strong staff administration                          | Limited incident, asset, household, and cross-workflow coordination            |
| Task-management apps         | Clear assignment and status                          | Limited household governance, approvals, signatures, and role boundaries       |
| Property-management software | Strong building, tenant, or community administration | Often not designed around a private household employer and its staff workflows |

The differentiation is a hypothesis to prove through comparative pilot evidence,
not a claim of established market superiority.

## Slide 11 — Go-to-Market

- Launch with high-trust pilot estates
- Target visible compliance and administrative pain
- Refine workflows through operational feedback
- Demonstrate measurable time and risk reduction
- Build case studies and reference customers
- Expand through referrals, estate networks, and trusted advisers

**Positioning:** risk reduction, operational clarity, and accountability—not
merely convenience software.

---

## Slide 12 — Milestones and Pilot Success

These are forward-looking targets.

- **30 September 2026:** First compliance workflow digitized
- **31 October 2026:** Three compliance workflows digitized
- **31 December 2026:** Five compliance workflows digitized
- Complete a pilot-ready workflow suite
- Begin initial estate onboarding after validation

**Initial pilot success targets**

- 100% of in-scope documents have a named owner and visible expiry status
- At least 50% fewer missed or late in-scope expiries during the measured pilot
- At least 30% less administrator time spent on the selected workflow
- At least 90% of in-scope incidents logged within one business day

Baselines must be recorded before each pilot. Targets become evidence only after
measurement against real operations.
